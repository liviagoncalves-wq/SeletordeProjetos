OUR STORY

Made of
passion


It all began with a cheesecake recipe and a family’s dream.

The story started with Evelyn Overton, who created her famous cheesecake recipe in the
1940s. In 1972, Evelyn and Oscar brought the business to Los Angeles.

In 1978, their son David Overton opened the first restaurant in Beverly Hills to showcase
the family’s heesecakes. From there, a dining experience was born—one known for its
variety, careful preparation, and desserts.

More than just serving food, the idea was always to create moments that people would want to experience again and again.

MORE THAN A RESTAURANT

A complete
experience


The concept combines a striking setting with an extensive menu and dishes prepared from scratch.
The company itself highlights three key aspects of the experience: fresh ingredients, skillful preparation,
and memorable meals.

It’s this combination that makes every visit feel different: you can choose a classic dish, try something new,
or simply save room for a slice of cheesecake.
